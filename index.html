About the Department

Electrical & Electronics Engineering

The EEE Department at PSG Institute of Technology is dedicated to nurturing engineers who can transform ideas into impactful solutions for modern power, automation, and electronics challenges. The department blends rigorous academic learning with practical exposure, research engagement, and societal relevance.

Our students are encouraged to explore emerging domains such as smart grids, renewable energy systems, power electronics, embedded systems, industrial automation, and sustainable energy technologies.
